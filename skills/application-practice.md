# Skill: application-practice

## Purpose
Test transfer by requiring a prediction, solution, design, classification, or scenario response before revealing the answer.

## Procedure
1. Select a concept and prerequisite-aware task from source evidence.
2. State constraints and success criteria without giving away the method.
3. Capture result, reasoning, and confidence.
4. Evaluate result and reasoning separately, cite the relevant source, and link errors to prerequisite concepts.
5. Schedule a delayed transfer attempt after correction.

## Output contract
Problem, answer/reasoning, evaluation, citation, feedback, and next action. Do not reveal solutions before the learner attempts the task.
