# Skill: confusion-review

## Purpose
Turn open confusion into targeted, source-located remediation.

## Procedure
1. Read open confusion items and due review history.
2. Rank by due date, severity, recurrence, topic/path importance, and source availability.
3. Show the exact source location and excerpt when available.
4. Let the learner choose reread, explain, compare, apply, or retry.
5. Require new evidence across an appropriate task before resolving; otherwise update review count and next date.
6. Merge duplicates by stable concept while preserving origin sessions.

## Output contract
A prioritized list, precise location to review, chosen remediation, evidence, and open/resolved outcome.
