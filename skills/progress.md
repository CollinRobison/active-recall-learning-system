# Skill: progress

## Purpose
Summarize evidence and recommend what to study next without overstating mastery.

## Files to read
Topic/path progress, sessions, confusion items, review due/history, source coverage, and config.

## Procedure
1. Aggregate recall, explanation, application, confidence, delayed review, and coverage evidence.
2. Identify trends, repeated confusion, over/under-confidence, due work, and uncovered objectives.
3. Rank recommendations with reasons and exact source locations.
4. State limitations and distinguish evidence from inference.
5. Write a snapshot only when configured; do not overwrite canonical notes.

## Output contract
Recent evidence, trends, open confusion, due reviews, coverage gaps, and actionable recommendations.
