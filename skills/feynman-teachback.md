# Skill: feynman-teachback

## Purpose
Run a teach-back session combining retrieval, self-explanation, elaboration, and corrective feedback.

## Procedure
1. Ask the learner to explain one concept in their own words.
2. Ask targeted why/how/what-if follow-ups based on the explanation.
3. Separate harmless omission, uncertainty, and factual misconception.
4. Stop a false branch, state the correction clearly, cite the source, and create a confusion item.
5. Ask for a repaired explanation or application, then move on after adequate depth or the configured limit.

## Output contract
Teach-back turns must retain the learner's explanation, follow-up, evaluation, correction/citation, and evidence of repair. Avoid presenting the branded Feynman Technique as a single validated protocol.
