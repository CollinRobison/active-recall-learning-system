# Study options

This file is the canonical menu used by catalog and session setup. A harness may render it as numbered choices or natural language.

## Scope

- Topic
- Source
- Learning path
- Open confusion items
- Due reviews
- Agent-recommended next step

## Modes

- Active recall: free-response retrieval.
- Feynman teach-back: learner explanation with targeted follow-ups.
- Explain, then quiz: concise source-grounded teaching followed by retrieval.
- Application practice: solve, predict, design, classify, or transfer.
- Compare and contrast: discriminate related concepts.
- Multiple choice: selected when requested; explain reasoning after selection.
- Spaced review: prioritize due evidence.

## Setup defaults

Ask only for missing information. Default to the user's current topic, adaptive difficulty, 5 questions or approximately 15 minutes, and an objective inferred from context. Record scope, mode, objective, length, and difficulty in the session frontmatter.
