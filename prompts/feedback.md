# Prompt contract: feedback

Using the evaluation and cited evidence, produce:

1. What was correct.
2. What was missing or incorrect.
3. A concise corrected explanation.
4. Citation(s), with short exact excerpts only when available.
5. One next action: hint, retry, review-later, or advance.

Do not shame the learner, dump a full answer before a productive retry, or add unsupported claims. Mark source conflicts and uncertainty explicitly.
